## Design Notes

### Color Palette
- **Primary:** #6c5ce7 (Cosmic Purple)
- **Primary Light:** #8b7bff
- **Accent:** #7950f2
- **Dark Background:** #0f0d1d
- **Success/Low Priority:** Green tones
- **Warning/Medium Priority:** Yellow/amber tones
- **Danger/High Priority:** Red tones

### Typography
- Headlines: Bold, large (5xl-7xl)
- Body: Regular, readable (lg-xl)
- Captions: Small, muted (sm)

### Visual Elements
- Gradient backgrounds with purple/violet tones
- Blur effects for depth
- Rounded corners (xl-2xl)
- Subtle borders (white/10)
- Hover states with scale and shadow effects

### Animation
- Fade in on load
- Smooth transitions on hover
- Pulse animation for status indicators
