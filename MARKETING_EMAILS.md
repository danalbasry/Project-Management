Welcome to FlowBoard!

You've just joined a community of people who believe productivity tools should be simple, beautiful, and instant.

**Here's what you can do right now:**

1. **Create your first card** - Click the "+" button in any column

### Email 2: Getting Started Tips (Send Day 2)

**Subject:** 3 ways to use FlowBoard (that you might not have thought of)

**Preview:** Beyond the basic to-do list

---

Hi again,

Now that you've had a day with FlowBoard, let me share some creative ways our users organize their work:

**1. The Weekly Sprint Board**

Columns: Backlog | This Week | In Progress | Done

Every Monday, drag items from Backlog to "This Week." Throughout the week, move them to Done. Friday satisfaction guaranteed.

**2. The Content Calendar**

Columns: Ideas | Drafting | Editing | Scheduled | Published

Perfect for bloggers, social media managers, and content creators. Watch your ideas become published work.

**3. The Decision Board**

Columns: Considering | Researching | Decided Yes | Decided No

Making a big decision? Lay out your options and move them as you gather information.

**Power user tip:** Use priorities to mark urgency within any system. High priority = this needs attention NOW.

What will you organize first?

Happy flowing,

The FlowBoard Team

### Email 3: Feature Deep Dive (Send Day 4)

**Subject:** Did you know FlowBoard can do this?

**Preview:** Features hiding in plain sight

---

Hey there,

Let's talk about some features you might have missed:

**Priority Levels**

Click any card and set its priority:
- **High** (red) - Urgent, do this first
- **Medium** (yellow) - Important, but not urgent
- **Low** (green) - Nice to have, when you get to it

These color-coded badges make scanning your board instant.

**Tags**

Add tags to categorize cards across columns:
- `#client-work` vs `#personal`
- `#quick-win` vs `#deep-work`
- `#bug` vs `#feature`

Tags help when you have mixed projects on one board.

**Keyboard Shortcuts**

- **ESC** - Close any modal instantly
- **Enter** - Submit forms without clicking

Small shortcuts. Big time savings.

**Column Management**

Don't like the default columns? Change them!
- Click the column header to rename
- Add new columns with the "+" button
- Delete columns you don't need

Make the board yours.

Try one new feature today,

The FlowBoard Team
